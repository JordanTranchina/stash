How to install:

  1. Drag the "Save to Stash" button above to your bookmarks bar
  2. If your bookmarks bar isn't visible, press Cmd+Shift+B
  3. Click it on any page to save - that's it!
Tip: Select text first, then click the bookmarklet to save just that highlight!
The bookmarklet opens Stash's quick-save window, which saves the page as whoever is signed in there. If you're signed out it asks you to sign in first rather than losing the page.
