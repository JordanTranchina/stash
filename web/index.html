General

Stash is a Progressive Web App, or PWA. A PWA is a website that works like an app. When you install it, Stash gets its own icon on your home screen. Tap the icon to open Stash right away — you don't need to open a browser first.

Set Up Share Sheet Save

Apple does not let installed web apps join the iOS share sheet. Use the Shortcut below instead. This token does not expire, so it's a one-time setup.

  1. Copy your save token above.
  2. On your iPhone or iPad, tap Get the Shortcut below.
  3. When Shortcuts asks for your save token, paste it in.
Get the Shortcut
Build it yourself instead
  1. Open the Shortcuts app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Build the "Save to Stash" Shortcut from your Stash project (see ios-shortcut/README.md).
  3. Copy your save token above and paste it into the Shortcut's header field.
  4. Turn on Show in Share Sheet for the Shortcut.
