How to install:

  1. Drag the button above to your bookmarks bar
  2. If your bookmarks bar isn't visible, press Cmd+Shift+B (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows)
  3. Click it on any page to save - that's it!
  4. Stay signed in to Stash in this browser; the bookmarklet saves as you
Tip: Select text first, then click the bookmarklet to save just that highlight!
How it works: The bookmarklet opens a small Stash window that does the save. Saves are attributed to whoever is signed in there, so the first click may ask you to sign in.
