The long slow death of the build step

Jane Dev@janedev
Jul 19, 2026
For a decade the build step was a fact of life. You wrote source, a tool chewed on it, and something else came out the other side. That bargain is quietly coming apart.

What changed

Native ES modules landed everywhere. Browsers stopped needing a bundler to load an app, and suddenly the only reason left to bundle was network round trips.
The second shift was speed. Once a compiler is written in a fast language, the difference between "build" and "no build" collapses to something under the threshold of noticing.

What it costs

None of this is free. You trade a build step for a much larger runtime surface, and the debugging story gets worse before it gets better.
